Burger Recipe

Description

This is a delicious homemade burger with a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape them into patties.
  3. Season the patties with salt and pepper on both sides.
  4. Place the patties on the grill and cook for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness.
  5. During the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty and close the grill lid to melt the cheese.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill for about 1 minute, or until lightly browned.
  7. Assemble the burgers by placing a lettuce leaf on the bottom half of each bun, followed by a cooked patty with melted cheese, tomato slices, onionslices, and pickles. Spread ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise on the top half of the bun.
  8. Serve the burgers immediately and enjoy!
